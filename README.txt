ABOVE — a little more looking up

Open Above.xcodeproj in Xcode 16 or later. Select an iPhone simulator and Run.
For a physical iPhone, select your development team under Signing & Capabilities,
connect your iPhone, and Run. Minimum iOS version: 17.

Features
• Month calendar with circular sky photographs and month navigation.
• Tap a day or Look up to open the camera. Tap the sky circle to record ten seconds and save a silent looping GIF. No photo library import.
• Tap a past date to take a photo; future dates are disabled.
• Tap a saved sky to view it, write an optional note, or delete it.
• Photos and notes are stored locally in the app Documents directory.

Design
Original bold system sans-serif, oversized lowercase month name, cream background,
black controls, generous spacing. Sky photos provide the color. No fixed personal
notes, streaks, or accounts. This prototype seeds six sample entries using three
AI-generated sky images once. Existing entries are preserved; samples can be deleted.

Validation
Unsigned iPhoneOS Debug build succeeded with Xcode 16.4.
Simulator launch and camera interaction were not verified in this sandbox.
This is a first functional prototype, not an App Store submission package.
