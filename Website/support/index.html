A few answers to help you capture a sky, keep a thought, and make Above feel like yours.

Getting startedCommon questionsTroubleshooting

One sky, one day.

  1. Open Month or Year and find today.
  2. Tap today’s empty date and allow camera access.
  3. Tap the sky circle to start a silent ten-second recording. Keep the app open while it finishes saving.
  4. Open your saved sky to add a thought.

Use the Month menu to choose Classic, Sky, or Poster. Tap Above to find palette, Photos-copy, and reminder settings.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Above works without login or a server. Your journal is saved locally on your iPhone.

Can I record a sky for yesterday?

New recordings are available only for today. You can still open, add notes to, or delete existing entries from earlier days.

Why won’t my palette change?

Tap Above, choose Background and Type colors, then tap Save in the Palette section. The preview changes before you save; leaving without saving keeps your existing palette.

Where are my recordings stored?

Recordings and notes are saved in Above. Turn on “Save a copy to Photos” in Above settings if you also want future recordings added to Photos. This setting does not export earlier entries or back up your notes.

How do daily reminders work?

Enable “Daily sky reminder” under Above and choose a time. The default is 6 PM. Saving today’s sky cancels its pending reminder. Reminders are scheduled locally, up to 60 days ahead, and refreshed when you use the app.

Why didn’t I receive a reminder?

Check that the reminder is enabled in Above, then check Settings → Notifications → Above. Focus, Scheduled Summary, and other iOS settings can affect delivery. Open Above again to refresh upcoming reminders. If you already saved today’s sky before your reminder time, no reminder is expected.

Can I recover a deleted sky?

Above does not currently offer an in-app trash or restore function. If you saved a copy to Photos, check Photos and its Recently Deleted album. Device-backup recovery depends on your Apple backup settings.

Will my journal move to a new phone?

Above does not provide its own cloud sync. A device backup or transfer may include app data, depending on your Apple settings. Keep Photos copies of skies you want to preserve, and check your backup before deleting the app or changing devices.

Does Above record sound or location?

No. Sky recordings are silent. Above does not request microphone or location permission.

If something feels stuck.

  1. Check camera access in iPhone Settings → Apps → Above.
  2. Check that your iPhone has available storage.
  3. Keep Above in the foreground until a recording has finished saving. Interrupted recordings may be discarded.
  4. Close and reopen the app, or restart your iPhone.

Keep your journal safe: avoid deleting Above as a troubleshooting step unless you have confirmed a backup or saved the copies you need.
