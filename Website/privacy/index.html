No account. No ads. No third-party analytics in the app.

01 / What the app stores

This policy applies to the Above iPhone sky journal app. Above saves the sky photos or silent animated GIFs you create, the dates of your entries, your notes, and preferences such as your chosen palette and reminder time.

This information is processed on your device. The app does not upload your journal to an Above server, create a user profile, or send your photos and notes to us. The app does not include advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

02 / Permissions, on your terms

Camera

Camera access lets you record the sky. Above records silent visual clips to create animated GIFs. It does not request microphone access or record audio.

Photos

If you enable “Save a copy to Photos,” Above requests permission to add your new sky recordings to your Apple Photos library. It does not request access to read your photo library. Copies saved to Photos are managed separately from your Above journal.

Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, Above requests notification permission and schedules reminders locally on your iPhone. There is no remote push-notification server.

You can change camera, Photos, and notification permissions in iPhone Settings. Optional Photos access and reminders are not required to keep a journal in Above.

03 / Storage, backups & deletion

Journal entries are stored in the app’s local storage until you delete them or remove the app. Open an entry and use its trash button to delete its saved media and note from Above.

Deleting an entry in Above does not delete copies previously saved to Apple Photos. Manage those copies, including Recently Deleted, in Photos.

Your iPhone’s backup settings may include app data in iCloud or computer backups. Your Photos settings may also sync exported copies with iCloud Photos. These services are controlled by Apple and your device settings; Above does not operate a separate cloud backup or sync service.

Deleting the app removes its local data, but does not remove separate Photos copies or existing device backups. Reinstalling or changing phones does not by itself guarantee recovery. Manage any backups through Apple’s settings and services.

04 / A gentle local reminder

Your reminder time is saved on your device. Above schedules reminders in advance and refreshes them when you use the app. When you save a real recording for today, the app updates its schedule to cancel today’s pending reminder.

You can turn reminders off in Above or iPhone Settings. Notification delivery is also affected by iOS settings, including Focus and notification summaries.

05 / Policy updates

If Above’s data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date. Any new permission will be requested through the appropriate iPhone permission prompt.
